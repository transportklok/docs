## What this page is for

Here you set how TransportKlok works for one team.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings**, choose the team and choose **Configuration and features**.
2. On the **Basic configuration** tab, change the settings, such as **File type for data exports**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you choose |
|------|-------------|
| **File type for data exports** | The file type of this team's exports: **Excel (xlsx)** or **CSV**. See [Exporting](/en/general/exports). |

## What happens next

- The setting only applies to this team.

## Limitations

- Settings for the whole organization are under [Basic configuration](/en/settings/organization/configuration/base).
