## What this page is for

With the **Routes** feature, your drivers register for a trip which delivery route they drove, how many stops they made and how many parcels they delivered.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization, or the owner or administrator of the team.

## How to use this page

Turn on the feature:

1. Open **Settings**, choose the team, choose **Configuration and features** and open the **Features** tab.
2. Activate the **Routes** feature. See [Features](/en/settings/team/configuration/features).
3. Open **Attributes** for the team and turn on **Route**, **Number of stops** and **Number of parcels** for the registrations where you want to ask for them. See [Attributes](/en/settings/team/attributes).

Manage routes:

1. For the team, in the menu under **Features**, choose **Routes**. You see **Delivery routes**.
2. At the top right, click `+`, fill in the **Name** and click **Create**.
3. Click **Edit** to change a name and then **Save**, or **Delete** to delete a route.
4. With **Trash** you see deleted routes. Click **Restore** to put a route back.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Name** (required) | The name of the route. Each name occurs once in a team. |

For a registration:

| Attribute | What the driver fills in |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Route** | The delivery route they drove, from the team's routes. |
| **Number of stops** | How many stops they made. |
| **Number of parcels** | How many parcels they delivered. |

## What happens next

- The routes are in the **Route** selection list for a registration, on the web and in the app.
- With the **Routes** filter under [Time registration](/en/management/hour-management/time-registrations/overview), you see the registrations of one route.

## Limitations

- You cannot reuse a route name that is already in the trash. You then see "A route with this name is already in the trash." Restore that route instead of creating a new one.
- The attributes are not on automatically. <Hint kind="grey">Turn them on per team under Attributes.</Hint>
