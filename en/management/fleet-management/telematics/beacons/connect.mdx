## What this page is for

On this page you link a beacon to the right type of object.

## What you can do here

- Choose whether you link to a worker or a trailer.
- Record the link.

## Screenshot

![Link beacon](/images/en/captures/management-druppels-koppelen.png)

## What you see on the page

- A choice for the type of link.
- Fields for worker or trailer.
- The **Connect** button.

## How to use this page

1. Open the link page from a beacon.
2. First choose the type you want to link.
3. Then select the right worker or trailer.
4. Click **Connect**.

## What happens next

- The beacon gets a clear link.
- You then see that link in the beacons overview.

## Good to know

- Always check that you are linking to the right object.
- A wrong link causes confusion in the overview later.
