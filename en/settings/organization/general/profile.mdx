## What this page is for

Here you record your organization's name, and the email address the invoices go to.

## Who can use this

- Everyone in the organization can view the details. Only an administrator can save them.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **General**.
2. On the **Profile** tab, fill in the details.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Name** | Your organization's name. |
| **Invoice email address** | The address the invoices from TransportKlok go to. |

## What happens next

- After **Save**, you see **Saved.** The new details apply to the next invoice.

## Limitations

- Only an administrator can change these details. For everyone else, **Save** is disabled.
- You set your organization's address under [Address](/en/settings/organization/general/address).
