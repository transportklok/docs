## What this page is for

Here you decide per team which activities and non-productive hours workers register on. You turn activities from the CAO on or off, and create your own activities.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** and choose the team, for example `Transport`.
2. Choose **Work activities** for productive hours, or **Non-productive** for hours such as leave, ATV and sickness.
3. On **Regular work**, use the switch to turn an activity from the CAO on or off. With **Edit** you change the **Code**, the **Attributes** and the **Variants**.
4. On **Other activities**, click **New activity** to create your own activity.
5. Fill in the fields and click **Create**.

## Fields

For your own activity:

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Name** | The name workers see when registering. |
| **Code** | The code, for example for the payroll export. |
| **Requestable absence** | Whether workers can request this activity as absence. |
| **Hour type** | The kind of hours. You only choose this when creating. |

For a variant of an activity, you fill in the **Name** and the **Code**.

## What happens next

- An activity that is on can be chosen by the team's workers when registering.
- A deleted own activity is in the trash. With **Restore from trash**, you put it back.

## Limitations

- The regular activities come from your CAO. You can turn them on and off, but not delete them. <Hint kind="yellow">Which regular activities there are depends on the CAO.</Hint>
