## What this page is for

Here you change a pay component, or delete it.

## Who can use this

- An administrator with full rights in the organization.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

Change a pay component:

1. Open **HR** → **Payroll administration** → **Additions & Deductions**.
2. Click **Edit** in the pay component's row.
3. Change the fields.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Delete a pay component:

1. Click **Delete** in the pay component's row.
2. In the **Delete pay component** window, check the pay component and the worker.
3. Click **Delete**.

## Fields

The **Edit pay component** form has the same fields as the create form, already filled in with the current values: **Worker**, **Payroll period**, **Type**, **Pay component** and **Value**. See [Create pay component](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-components/create).

## What happens next

- After **Save changes**, the changed pay component is in the overview.
- After **Delete**, the pay component disappears from the overview right away. <Hint kind="red">You cannot restore a deleted pay component.</Hint>
- The period's payroll run includes the change. If that run is a draft or locked, it is recalculated.
- If the run has already been processed, it is checked again for corrections. If a difference remains with what was already processed, it gets the status **Corrected**. If the difference disappears, it goes back to **Processed**. See [Corrections on a payroll run](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-runs/corrections).

## Limitations

- Even for a processed payroll run, you can still change or delete a pay component. The change then comes as a correction in a following processing.
