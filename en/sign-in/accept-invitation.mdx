## What this page is for

Your employer invites you to TransportKlok. Here you read how to accept the invitation and create your account.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who has received an invitation by email or an invitation link.

## How to use this page

With an invitation by email:

1. Open the email and click **Accept Invitation**.
2. If you do not have an account yet, fill in your name and a password, agree to the terms and click **Complete**.
3. If you already have an account, log in. The invitation is then accepted.

With an invitation link:

1. Open the link you received from your employer.
2. Under **Open invitation**, fill in your **Email Address** and name, and optionally your date of birth and gender.
3. Click **Continue** and follow the steps above.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Email Address** | The address you want to log in with. With an email invitation, it is already filled in. |
| Name | Your initials, first name, optionally a prefix and last name. |
| **Password**, **Confirm password** | At least 8 characters, with at least 1 capital letter, 1 digit and 1 special character. |

## What happens next

- You receive an email to confirm your email address. See [Confirm email address](/en/sign-in/confirm-email).
- After that, you work in your employer's organization.

## Limitations

- Log in with the same email address the invitation was sent to. With a different account, the invitation does not work.
- Logging in only works in the Chrome browser, or in the TransportKlok app.
