## What this page is for

If two-factor authentication is on for your account, TransportKlok also asks for a code after your password. Here you read how that works.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who has turned on two-factor authentication. See [Two-factor authentication](/en/settings/user/authentication-and-security/two-factor-authentication).

## How to use this page

1. Log in with your email address and password.
2. On the **Two-factor authentication** screen, open the authenticator app on your phone.
3. Fill in the **Code** from the app and click **Log in**.
4. If you do not have your phone at hand, click **Use a recovery code** and fill in one of your recovery codes.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Code** | The 6-digit code from your authenticator app. |
| **Recovery code** | One of the recovery codes you saved when turning it on. |

## What happens next

- With a correct code, you go to TransportKlok.
- With **Use an authentication code**, you go back from the recovery code to the code from the app.

## Limitations

- Every recovery code works once. <Hint kind="red">If you have lost your phone and your recovery codes, you can no longer get into your account. Create new recovery codes in time under [Two-factor authentication](/en/settings/user/authentication-and-security/two-factor-authentication).</Hint>
- After a few wrong attempts, you have to wait a moment before you try again.
