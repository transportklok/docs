## What this page is for

After creating your account, you confirm your email address. Only then do you work in TransportKlok.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who has created a new account.

## How to use this page

1. Open the email TransportKlok sent you after you created your account.
2. Click the link in the email.
3. If you do not have the email, click **Resend verification email** on the **Email Verification** screen.

## What happens next

- Your email address is confirmed and you go to TransportKlok.

## Limitations

- As long as your email address is not confirmed, you keep seeing the **Email Verification** screen after logging in.
