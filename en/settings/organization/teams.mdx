## What this page is for

A team is a group of workers with its own settings, such as activities and templates. Here you create teams and open their settings.

## Screenshot

![Teams](/images/en/captures/instellingen-organisatie-teams.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.
- You see teams if your organization has more than one team and the subscription is **Plus** or **Pro**. <Hint kind="yellow">You can create, delete or restore a team with an active **Plus** or **Pro** subscription or during the trial period.</Hint>

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Teams**.
2. Click **Settings** in a team's row to open the team settings.
3. Click **Add new team** to create a team. Fill in the **Name** and click **Create**.
4. Click **Delete** to remove a team, and confirm in the **Delete team** window.

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Name** | The team's name. |
| **Number of workers** | How many workers belong to the team. |
| **Phone number** | The team's phone number. |

For a new team, you only fill in the **Name**. It must be unique within your organization.

## What happens next

- After **Create**, you go to the new team's settings. The team gets the default settings of your CAO.
- A deleted team is in the **Trash**. With **Restore**, you bring it back.

## Limitations

- Which workers belong to a team is set on the worker. See [Teams of a worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/teams).
