## What this page is for

A worker belongs to one or more teams. Here you read how to set that membership and where to see it.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization. You only see and choose the teams you manage.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Workers**.
2. In the worker's row, choose **Actions** → `Wijzig team(s)`. You go to the **Teams** tab of [Edit worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/edit).
3. Tick or untick the teams. With **Select all** you choose all teams.
4. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Teams** | The worker's teams. At least one. |

## What happens next

- In the overview, the teams are in the **Teams** column.
- On [View worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/details), each team shows since when the worker has been a member.

## Limitations

- You only see `Wijzig team(s)` and the **Teams** tab if your organization has more than one team.
- A worker does not get a separate role per team. The kind of worker (**Employee**, **Self-employed (ZZP)** or **Charter**) follows from the contract.
