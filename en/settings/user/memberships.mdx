## What this page is for

If you belong to more than one organization, you switch organization here. You also accept open invitations here.

## Who can use this

- Every user.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Organizations and teams**.
2. Click **Open** next to the organization you want to work in.
3. If there is an invitation, click **Accept**.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| Organization | The organizations you belong to, with your role. |
| Invitations | Invitations you have not accepted yet. |

## What happens next

- After **Open**, you work in that organization.
- After **Accept**, you belong to the new organization.

## Limitations

- You do not leave an organization here. An administrator does that under [Administrators](/en/settings/organization/administrators).
