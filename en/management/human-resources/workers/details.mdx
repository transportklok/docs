## What this page is for

Here you view everything that has been recorded about a worker: the personal details, the contracts and the teams.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Workers**.
2. Click the icon in front of the line, or choose **Actions** → `Details weergeven`.
3. View the **Details**, **Contracts** and **Teams** tabs.
4. Click **Changes** to change the details of the open tab.

## Fields

| Tab | What you see |
|---------|------------|
| **Details** | **Email Address**, **Initials**, **First name**, **Prefix**, **Last name**, **Employee number**, **Date of birth** and **Gender**. If telematics is on, also **Tachograph driver code**, **Home address** and **Locations**. |
| **Contracts** | Per contract the **Type**, the **Start**, the **End** and the **Status**: **New**, **Current**, **Future** or **Former**. |
| **Teams** | Per team the **Name** and since when the worker has been a member. You see this tab if your organization has more than one team. |

## What happens next

- With **Changes** you go to [Edit worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/edit), on the same tab.

## Limitations

- This page does not show time registrations. You find those under [Time registration](/en/management/hour-management/time-registrations/overview).
