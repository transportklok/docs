## What this page is for

Here you read how to log in to the TransportKlok app on your phone, and how the app then uses your face or fingerprint.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-inloggen.png" alt="Logging in to the app" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker with a TransportKlok account. If you do not have an account yet, first accept your employer's invitation. See [Accept invitation](/en/sign-in/accept-invitation).

## How to use this page

Log in:

1. Open the TransportKlok app. You see **Welcome back**.
2. Fill in your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Log in**.
3. If your phone has Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint set up, the phone now asks for it. Look at your phone or put your finger on the sensor.
4. If two-factor authentication is on, fill in the code from your authenticator app and tap **Log in**. If you do not have the app at hand, tap `Use a recovery code` and fill in a recovery code.

Forgot your password:

1. Tap **Forgot your password?**.
2. Fill in your email address and tap **Email password reset link**.
3. Open the email and set a new password. Then tap **Back to login** in the app.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Email** | The email address of your TransportKlok account. |
| **Password** | Your password. |

## What happens next

- After logging in, the app asks once whether you want push notifications. See [Push notifications](/en/workforce/app/push-notifications). After that, you see **Home**. See [Home](/en/workforce/app/home).
- If you have Face ID or a fingerprint, the app also asks for it when you open the app again later and when your session is renewed. You then do not have to fill in your password again.

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-opstarten-face-id.png" alt="Face ID when opening the app" width="320" />

- If you see **Faster and more secure login**, tap `Gezichts-ID inschakelen` (or the name of your fingerprint) to turn this on, or tap **Skip**.
- If you have not yet confirmed your email address, the app asks for it. Click the link in the email and tap **Refresh**, or tap **Resend verification email**.

## Limitations

- After a few wrong attempts, you see "Too many login attempts. Please wait 1 minute."
- If you see `Sessie niet hersteld` when opening, tap `Opnieuw inloggen` and log in with your email address and password.
- If you have not set up Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint, you only log in with your password. <Hint kind="grey">If you later turn on Face ID or a fingerprint on your phone, the app uses it from the next time you log in.</Hint>
