## What this page is for

Here you view workers' time registrations and use filters to find the right registrations.

## What you can do here

- View registrations.
- Create a new registration.
- Export.
- Filter by worker, activity, vehicle, trailer and other items.

## Screenshot

![Time registration overview](/images/nl/captures/management-tijdregistratie-overzicht.png)

## What you see on the page

- A period choice.
- Buttons such as **New registration** and **Export**.
- Filters for workers, activities, vehicles, trailers and clients.

## How to use this page

1. First choose the right period.
2. Then use filters to narrow the overview.
3. Open **New registration** if you want to enter a new registration right away.

## What happens next

- You only see the registrations that fit your selection.
- You also start new registrations from here.

## Good to know

- Always check the period first, so you are working in the right week.
- Use filters to find the right registration or worker faster.
