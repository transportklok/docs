## What this page is for

Here you choose your organization's subscription and see what it costs. You pay per active worker per month, with one invoice for the whole organization.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Subscription**.
2. Under **Plan**, choose the subscription.
3. During the trial period, click **Start subscription**. If you already have a subscription, choose **Change immediately** or **Change next cycle**.
4. On the **Components** tab, you see which functions and modules are in your subscription.

## Fields

| Plan | What is included |
|------|--------------|
| **Start** | Register work and leave hours and create exports, such as hour accountability reports and Excel or CSV overviews. |
| **Plus** | Everything in Start, with process registration, extra modules such as Fleet management and Client management, and teams. |
| **Pro** | Everything in Plus, with integrations, API access, webhooks and custom attributes. |

Each plan shows the price per worker.

## What happens next

- After **Start subscription**, you arrange payment through a mandate. See [Mandate](/en/settings/organization/plan-and-billing/payment-method).
- With a subscription, you see the current plan, the **Price**, the status (**Free trial**, **Active** or **Inactive**) and when it is renewed.

## Limitations

- You move to a cheaper plan with **Change next cycle**. <Hint kind="grey">**Change immediately** is then disabled.</Hint>
- During the trial period, you see the end date at the top of the page.
- If you want to cancel your subscription, contact TransportKlok.
