## What this page is for

Here you find all your invoices from TransportKlok, with their status.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Mandate and invoicing**.
2. Open the **Invoices** tab.
3. Click **Download** next to an invoice.

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Amount** | The amount of the invoice. |
| **Status** | For example **Paid**, **Pending**, **Failed** or **Direct debit pending**. |
| **Processed on** | When the payment was processed. |

## What happens next

- If a payment has failed or expired, the invoice shows **Reissue direct debit** or **Retry payment request**.

## Limitations

- You set the billing address under [Address](/en/settings/organization/general/address), and the email address for invoices under [Profile](/en/settings/organization/general/profile).
