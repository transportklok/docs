## What this page is for

With this multi-step page you enter a new time registration for a worker.

## What you can do here

- Choose a worker.
- Choose the type of registration.
- Fill in a date and further registration details.

## Screenshot

![Create time registration](/images/en/captures/management-tijdregistratie-aanmaken.png)

## What you see on the page

- Steps such as **Worker**, **Workday / pattern** and **Activities**.
- Fields for type, date and further input.

## How to use this page

1. Choose the worker you are registering for.
2. Choose the right registration type.
3. Fill in the date.
4. Complete the next steps one by one.
5. Save the registration once all steps are complete.

## What happens next

- The new registration is added to the hours overview.
- You can then reopen the registration later to check or change it.

## Good to know

- Work calmly, step by step, to prevent mistakes.
- Choose the registration type carefully, because it determines which further steps you see.
