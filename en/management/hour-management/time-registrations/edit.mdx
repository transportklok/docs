## What this page is for

With this page you change an existing time registration. The fields are very similar to the create steps.

## What you can do here

- Correct or supplement existing registration details.

## Screenshot

![Edit time registration](/images/en/captures/management-tijdregistratie-aanmaken.png)

## What you see on the page

- A form that looks like the create page, but with existing values.

## How to use this page

1. Open the existing registration from the overview.
2. Only change the details that need to change.
3. Check the registration once more.
4. Save the change.

## What happens next

- The updated registration is visible in the overview and in further processing.

## Good to know

- In practice, use an existing registration from the overview as your starting point.
