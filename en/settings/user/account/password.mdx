## What this page is for

Here you change your password.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Open the **Password** tab.
3. Fill in your **Current password**, a **New password** and **Confirm password**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Current password** | Your current password. |
| **New password**, **Confirm password** | Your new password, twice. |

## What happens next

- You now log in with the new password.

## Limitations

- If you think someone else is in your account, change your password and log out of your other sessions. See [Web sessions](/en/settings/user/authentication-and-security/sessions).
