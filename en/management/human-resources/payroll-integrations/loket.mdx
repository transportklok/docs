## What this page is for

With the Loket.nl integration, you send workers, contracts and processed payroll runs to Loket.nl.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization, with a Loket.nl account that has access to the employer.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Create an account for **Loket.nl** and log in to Loket.nl. See [Accounts](/en/settings/organization/integrations/accounts).
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Loket.nl**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. On **Sync behavior**, turn on the switches for what you want to send, such as **Send payroll runs**, and save.

## Fields

Besides **Account** and **Name**, the integration asks for no settings. The employer follows from the account you log in with.

## What happens next

- If you create or change a worker or contract, TransportKlok sends that data to Loket.nl.
- When a payroll run is **Sent**, TransportKlok sends the hours and pay components of that period per worker to Loket.nl. Existing data for that period is overwritten.

## Limitations

- Only pay components with a Loket.nl code are sent. <Hint kind="yellow">Which codes are set depends on how the integration is set up for your organization. After the first submission, check in Loket.nl that the hours and pay components are there.</Hint>
- The integration fetches nothing from Loket.nl.
