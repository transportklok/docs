## What this page is for

Here you manage the clients your organization works for.

## What you can do here

- View clients.
- Add a new client.
- Open existing clients for further actions.

## Screenshot

![Clients overview](/images/en/captures/management-opdrachtgevers-overzicht.png)

## What you see on the page

- The **New client** button.
- A table with reference, name, contact person, email address and phone number.
- An explanation above the table.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Clients**.
2. First check whether the client already exists.
3. Use **New client** if you want to add a new relation.

## What happens next

- New clients appear in the overview.
- Existing clients can later be used in time registrations and other processes.

## Good to know

- Work carefully here, because clients can also be chosen in other parts.
- Check the contact details carefully.
