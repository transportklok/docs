## What this page is for

Here you change the email address you log in with.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Account**.
2. On the **Email Address** tab, change your address.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Email Address** | The address you log in with and receive emails at. |

## What happens next

- You now log in with the new email address.
