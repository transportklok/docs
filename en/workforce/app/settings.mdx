## What this page is for

In **Settings** you manage your profile, your account, the app's language and your account's security.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-instellingen.png" alt="Settings in the app" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker who uses the app.

## How to use this page

1. At the bottom, tap **Settings**.
2. Tap the item you want to change.
3. Change it and tap **Save**.

## Fields

| Item | What you do there |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Profile** | Choose or remove your **Profile photo**, and change your **Name**. |
| **Email Address** | Change the email address you log in with. |
| **Change password** | Fill in and confirm your **Current password** and a new **Password**. |
| **Language** | Choose the app's language: Nederlands, English, Deutsch, Polski or Română. |
| **Web sessions** | With **Log out of other browser sessions**, you log out everywhere else. |
| **Devices** | The phones that have access to your account. With **Revoke**, you remove a phone. |
| **Two-factor authentication** | With **Enable**, you turn on two-factor authentication. |
| **Organization and teams** | Choose a different organization. See [Organizations and invitations](/en/workforce/app/organizations). |
| **Apply update** | Start a new version of the app that has already been downloaded now. |
| **Log out** | Log out on this phone. |

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-instellingen-apparaten.png" alt="Devices" width="320" />

## What happens next

- After **Save**, you see the change right away in the app.
- After **Log out**, you see **Welcome back** again.

## Limitations

- A new password has at least 8 characters, with at least 1 capital letter, 1 digit and 1 special character.
- For **Web sessions**, **Devices** and **Two-factor authentication**, you first confirm your identity with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint. <Hint kind="yellow">If you have not set that up on your phone, you see "Biometric verification required" and you cannot change these items in the app.</Hint>
- You only see **Apply update** if a new version is ready.
