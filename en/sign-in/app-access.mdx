## What this page is for

Some external apps ask for access to your TransportKlok account. Here you read what you see and how to give permission.

## Who can use this

- Everyone with a TransportKlok account.

## How to use this page

1. In the external app, start linking with TransportKlok. You arrive at the **Authorize application** screen.
2. Check the **Application**, which account you are **Signed in as** and the **Requested permissions**.
3. Under **Granted organizations**, choose which organizations the app gets access to.
4. Click **Authorize**, or **Cancel** if you do not want to give access.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| **Application** | The app asking for access, with a description. |
| **Signed in as** | The account you give access with. |
| **Requested permissions** | What the app wants to be able to do. |
| **Granted organizations** | The organizations the app gets access to. |

## What happens next

- After **Authorize**, you go back to the app, which now has access.
- The app is under **Settings** → **Apps**. There you revoke the access again. See [Apps](/en/settings/user/apps).

## Limitations

- <Hint kind="red">Only give access to apps you trust. The app can do what is listed under **Requested permissions**.</Hint>
