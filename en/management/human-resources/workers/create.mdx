## What this page is for

Here you add a worker: the person, the contract and the teams the worker belongs to.

## Screenshot

![Create worker](/images/en/captures/management-personeel-aanmaken.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Workers** and click **New worker**.
2. On the **Details** tab, choose whether the worker gets an account: `Met gebruikersaccount` or `Zonder gebruikersaccount`.
3. Fill in the person's details.
4. Open the **Contracts** tab, click `+` and fill in the contract.
5. Open the **Teams** tab and tick the teams.
6. Click **New worker**.

## Fields

**Details** tab:

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| `Met gebruikersaccount` / `Zonder gebruikersaccount` (required) | With an account, the worker can log in and record data themselves. Without an account, you only record data about the worker. |
| **Email** | Only with an account, and then required. The worker receives the invitation at this address. |
| **Name** (required) | Initials, first name, optionally a prefix and last name. The prefix is not required. |
| **Employee number** | A unique number within your organization. |
| **Date of birth** | The date of birth. |
| Gender | **Male**, **Female** or **Other**. |
| **Tachograph driver code** | The code of the driver's driver card. <Hint kind="grey">You see this field, **Home address** and **Locations** if telematics is on for your organization.</Hint> |
| **Home address** | The home address. |
| **Locations** | Per kind of location, such as `Standplaatsen` and `Kantoren`, which locations apply to this worker. |

**Contracts** tab, per contract:

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Employment** (required) | **Employee**, **Self-employed (ZZP)** or **Charter**. |
| **Start** (required) | The date on which the contract begins. |
| **End** | The date on which the contract ends. Empty means ongoing. |
| **Contract type** | For an employee: **On-call worker**, **Part-time worker** or **Full-time worker**. |
| **Contract hours type** | **Fixed hours** or **Min-max hours**. |
| **Contract hours** | The number of hours per week. <Hint kind="yellow">A full-time worker always has 40 hours, an on-call worker 0. What the hours mean for the pay calculation depends on the CAO or the contract setting.</Hint> |

**Teams** tab: tick the teams. With **Select all** you choose all teams. You choose from the teams you manage, and at least one.

## What happens next

- You return to the overview with the new worker.
- With an account, the worker receives an email with **Accept Invitation**. As long as it has not been accepted, the invitation is listed under [Invitations](/en/management/human-resources/workers/invitations).

## Limitations

- A worker needs at least one contract. Contracts may not overlap.
- You cannot use an email address that already has an open invitation or already belongs to your organization.
