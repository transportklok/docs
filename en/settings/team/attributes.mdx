## What this page is for

Attributes are extra details on a time registration, such as the vehicle, the trailer or the odometer reading. Here you decide which attributes a team uses.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings**, choose the team and choose **Attributes**.
2. Choose the tab: **Work activities**, **Productive time entries** or **Versions**.
3. Click **Add attribute** and choose an attribute in the window. Click **Add**.
4. Per activity, you turn attributes on or off under **Work activities** → **Edit** → **Attributes**.

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Name** | The attribute, for example **Vehicle** or **Odometer (start)**. |
| **Description** | What the worker fills in. |
| **Calculable** | Whether the attribute counts in calculations. <Hint kind="yellow">What a calculable attribute does in the payroll calculation depends on the CAO or the contract setting.</Hint> |

## What happens next

- An added attribute is asked of the worker whenever they register.

## Limitations

- You choose from the attributes TransportKlok offers. You cannot create or delete an attribute yourself.
- On the **Versions** tab, you do not add attributes.
