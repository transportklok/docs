## What this page is for

Here you record a team's address. This address appears on the team's invoices.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** and choose the team.
2. Choose **General** and open the **Address** tab.
3. Fill in the address and click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Street**, **Postal code**, **City**, **Phone number** | The team's address, used on invoices. |

## What happens next

- The team uses this address on invoices. Without its own address, the team uses the organization's address. See [Address](/en/settings/organization/general/address).

## Limitations

- You only see **General** if your organization has more than one team.
