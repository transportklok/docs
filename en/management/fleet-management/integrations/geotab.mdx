## What this page is for

With the Geotab integration, TransportKlok fetches vehicles and positions from Geotab.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.
- The organization needs the **Fleet management** module, and telematics must be on. See [Integrations](/en/settings/organization/integrations/instances).

## How to use this page

1. Under **Accounts**, create an account for **Geotab** with a **Token** from Geotab.
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Geotab**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.

## Fields

After connecting, the integration asks for no settings.

## What happens next

| Function | Runs automatically |
|---------|---------------|
| **Synchronize vehicles** | every night |
| **Synchronize drivers** | every night |
| **Synchronize vehicle assignments** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Receive telemetry** | every quarter of an hour |

- Vehicles are linked on license plate. An active vehicle takes precedence over a deleted vehicle.
- Positions appear on the [Map](/en/management/fleet-management/map).

## Limitations

- The integration only fetches data from Geotab. It does not send anything back.
