## What this page is for

Here you see in which browsers you are logged in, and you log out of the others.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Authentication and security**.
2. On the **Web sessions** tab, click **Log out of other browser sessions**.
3. Fill in your **Password** and confirm.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| Session | A browser in which you are logged in. **This device** is the browser you are working in now. |
| Last active | When the session was last active. |

## What happens next

- You are logged out of all other browsers. The browser you are working in now stays logged in.
