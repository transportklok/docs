## What this page is for

With the Linqo integration, TransportKlok fetches vehicles and positions from Linqo.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.
- The organization needs the **Fleet management** module, and telematics must be on. See [Integrations](/en/settings/organization/integrations/instances).

## How to use this page

1. Under **Accounts**, create an account for **Linqo** with a **Token** from Linqo.
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Linqo**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. Fill in the settings and click **Save settings**.

## Fields

On the settings page you fill in:

| Setting | What you fill in |
|------------|--------------|
| **Link Linqo tachograph code to drivers** | Turn this on if the tachograph codes in Linqo equal the tachograph codes of your workers. |

## What happens next

| Function | Runs automatically |
|---------|---------------|
| **Synchronize vehicles** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Synchronize drivers** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Synchronize vehicle assignments** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Receive telemetry** | every quarter of an hour |

- Vehicles are linked on the chassis number (VIN). Drivers are linked on tachograph code.
- Positions appear on the [Map](/en/management/fleet-management/map).

## Limitations

- The integration only fetches data from Linqo. It does not send anything back.
