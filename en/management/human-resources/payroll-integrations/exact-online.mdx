## What this page is for

With the Exact Online integration, you send workers and contracts to Exact Online.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization, with an Exact Online account that has access to the administration.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Create an account for **Exact Online** and log in to Exact Online. See [Accounts](/en/settings/organization/integrations/accounts).
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Exact Online**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. On **Sync behavior**, turn on the switches for what you want to send and save.

## Fields

Besides **Account** and **Name**, the integration asks for no settings. The administration follows from the account you log in with.

## What happens next

- If you create or change a worker or contract, TransportKlok sends that data to Exact Online.

## Limitations

- The integration does not send payroll runs. For a payroll run, the **Complete** button remains. For the payout, use the [exports of a payroll run](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-runs/exports).
- The integration fetches nothing from Exact Online.
