## What this page is for

**Home** is the first screen after logging in. Here you start a registration and see how much you have worked.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-dashboard.png" alt="Home in the app" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker who uses the app.

## How to use this page

1. At the bottom, tap **Home**.
2. Under **Quick actions**, choose what you want to do:
   - **Register leave**. See [Register leave](/en/workforce/app/register-leave).
   - **Register work**. See [Register work](/en/workforce/app/register-work).
   - **Start timer**. See [Timer](/en/workforce/app/timer).
3. Under **Statistics**, choose the period, for example **This month**.
4. Under **Rejected registrations**, see whether your employer has rejected registrations.

## Fields

| Item | What you see |
|-----------|------------|
| **Days worked** | The number of days you worked in the chosen period. |
| **Hours worked** | Your hours worked in the chosen period. |
| **Break time** | Your breaks in the chosen period. |
| **Rejected registrations** | Your registrations that have been rejected, or "You have no rejected registrations." |

At the bottom is the menu bar:

| Button | What you do there |
|------|-----------------|
| **Home** | Back to this screen. |
| **Overview** | Your registrations per week. See [Overview](/en/workforce/app/overview). |
| `+` | Create a new registration. See [Register work](/en/workforce/app/register-work). |
| **Map** | Colleagues, vehicles and trailers on the map. See [Map](/en/workforce/app/map). |
| **Settings** | Your profile, account and security. See [Settings](/en/workforce/app/settings). |

## What happens next

- You change a rejected registration through [Overview](/en/workforce/app/overview).

## Limitations

- Here you only see your own hours and registrations.
- You only see **Start timer** if your organization takes part in the beta program.
