## What this page is for

Here you manage all trailers. You see right away which trailers are known and which actions are available.

## What you can do here

- View trailers.
- Add a trailer.
- Delete a trailer.

## Screenshot

![Trailers overview](/images/en/captures/management-trailers-overzicht.png)

## What you see on the page

- The **New vehicle** button for entering a new trailer.
- A table with license plate, country and MOT expiry date.
- Actions per trailer row.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Trailers** in Fleet management.
2. Find the right trailer in the table.
3. Open the create page if you want to enter a new trailer.
4. Use the actions available per row for further follow-up.

## What happens next

- New trailers appear in the overview.
- Deleted trailers disappear from the active list.

## Good to know

- Pay particular attention to the license plate and expiry date.
- In this overview you quickly see whether trailer details are still complete.
