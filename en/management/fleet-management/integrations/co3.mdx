## What this page is for

With the CO3 integration, TransportKlok fetches vehicles and positions from CO3.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.
- The organization needs the **Fleet management** module, and telematics must be on. See [Integrations](/en/settings/organization/integrations/instances).

## How to use this page

1. Under **Accounts**, create an account for **CO3** with a **Client ID** and **Client secret** from CO3.
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **CO3**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. Fill in the settings and click **Save settings**.

## Fields

On the settings page you fill in:

| Setting | What you fill in |
|------------|--------------|
| **CO3 sharing-company ID** | The code of the CO3 company that shares the vehicles with your account. The synchronization only starts once this is filled in. |

## What happens next

| Function | Runs automatically |
|---------|---------------|
| **Synchronize vehicles** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Receive telemetry** | every quarter of an hour |

- Vehicles are linked to your existing vehicles by chassis number (VIN) and license plate. CO3 does not create new vehicles itself.
- Positions appear on the [Map](/en/management/fleet-management/map).
- CO3 does not fetch drivers or vehicle assignments. Earlier GPS positions are fetched as well.

## Limitations

- The integration only fetches data from CO3. It does not send anything back.
