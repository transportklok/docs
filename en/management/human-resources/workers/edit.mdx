## What this page is for

Here you change a worker's details, contracts and teams.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Workers**.
2. Click **Edit** in the worker's row. Through **Actions**, `Wijzig contracten` or `Wijzig team(s)` takes you straight to that tab.
3. Change the fields on **Details**, **Contracts** or **Teams**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Fields

The fields are the same as in [Create worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/create), already filled in with the current values.

- If the worker already has an account, you no longer see the choice for an account and the email address.
- If an invitation is still open, you can change the email address. <Hint kind="grey">The old invitation expires and a new one is sent to the new address.</Hint>

## What happens next

- You return to the overview with the changed details.

## Limitations

- For a contract that has already started, you can no longer change the start, the employment or an end date in the past. You also cannot delete such a contract.
- The contracts must continue to cover all of the worker's time registrations.
- You remove a worker from your organization in the overview with **Delete**. See [Workers](/en/management/human-resources/workers/overview).
