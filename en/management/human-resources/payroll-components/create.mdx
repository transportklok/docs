## What this page is for

Here you add an amount to the pay, or deduct an amount from it, for one worker and one payroll period.

## Screenshot

![Create pay component](/images/nl/captures/management-aanvullingen-inhoudingen-aanmaken.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator with full rights in the organization.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Payroll administration** → **Additions & Deductions**.
2. Click **New pay component**.
3. Choose the **Worker**.
4. Choose the **Payroll period**.
5. Under **Type**, choose whether it is an **Addition** or a **Deduction**.
6. Choose the **Pay component**.
7. Fill in the **Value**.
8. Click **Save pay component**.

## Fields

All fields are required.

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Worker** | The worker who receives or gives up the amount. |
| **Payroll period** | The period in which the amount counts, for example `Periode 2026-11`. You choose from the current period and the periods for which a payroll run already exists. |
| **Type** | **Addition** adds the amount, **Deduction** deducts it. **Addition** is preselected. |
| **Pay component** | The kind of amount, for example **Mileage allowance** or **Advance** for an addition and **Fines** for a deduction. <Hint kind="yellow">Which pay components you see depends on the type and on your organization's settings.</Hint> |
| **Value** | The amount in euros, with two decimals. <Hint kind="grey">A negative amount lowers the total.</Hint> |

## What happens next

- You return to the overview, where the new pay component is listed.
- The payroll run of that period includes the amount. See [Additions and deductions](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-components/overview) for what happens to the run.

## Limitations

- A pay component applies to one worker and one period. For a fixed amount every period, you create a pay component per period.
- If you leave a required field empty, the pay component is not saved and you see a message at that field.
