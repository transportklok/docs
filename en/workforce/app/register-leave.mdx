## What this page is for

Here you register hours in which you did not work, such as leave, sickness, a public holiday or rest time.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-verlof-uren.png" alt="Register non-productive hours" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker who uses the app.

## How to use this page

1. On **Home**, tap **Register leave**. **Non-productive (leave, layover (overstaan) or rest period)** is already chosen.
2. Choose the **Date** and tap **Next**.
3. Under **Team**, choose your team and tap **Next**.
4. Under **Non-productive hours**, tap **Add activity** and choose the kind of hours.
5. Fill in the times and add your **Signature**.
6. Tap **Complete**.

## Fields

| Kind of hours | When you choose this |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Working time reduction (ATV)** | Working time reduction (ATV). |
| **Leave** | Regular leave. |
| **Special leave** | Leave for a special event. |
| **Sick** | You were sick. |
| **Public holiday** | A recognized public holiday. |
| **Unpaid leave** | Leave without pay. |
| **Layover day (overstaan)** | Waiting time that is not your fault. |
| **On-call duty** | You were on call. |
| **Rest period** | Your mandatory rest period. |

<Hint kind="grey">Which kinds you see depends on what your employer has set for your team.</Hint>

## What happens next

- The registration is in [Overview](/en/workforce/app/overview) and waits for your employer's approval.

## Limitations

- If the pay period of that day has already been processed, you can no longer register hours for it.
