## What this page is for

With this page you enter a new time registration step by step.

## What you can do here

- Choose a date.
- Choose a workday or pattern.
- Fill in activities.

## Screenshot

![Create time registration](/images/en/captures/workforce-tijdregistratie-aanmaken.png)

## What you see on the page

- A multi-step form.
- Clear steps in the input.
- Fields that change per step.

## How to use this page

1. Open **New registration**.
2. Fill in the steps in the order of the screen.
3. Check your input per step.
4. Save the registration when everything is correct.

## What happens next

- Your registration appears in your overview.
- Your totals for the period are updated.

## Good to know

- Work calmly, step by step.
- Check the date and the entered activities extra carefully.
