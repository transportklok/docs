## What this page is for

With this page you change an existing registration.

## What you can do here

- Correct details.
- Supplement a registration.

## Screenshot

![Edit time registration](/images/en/captures/workforce-tijdregistratie-bewerken.png)

## What you see on the page

- A form with existing values.
- Input that looks like the create steps.

## How to use this page

1. Open the existing registration.
2. Only change the fields that need to change.
3. Check everything once more.
4. Save the change.

## What happens next

- Your changed registration then appears with the new details in the overview.

## Good to know

- Especially check the date, times and activities before you save.
