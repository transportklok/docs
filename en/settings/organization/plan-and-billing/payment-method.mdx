## What this page is for

Here you manage the mandate with which TransportKlok charges the subscription.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Mandate and invoicing**.
2. On the **Mandate** tab, you see the account holder, the account number and when the mandate was signed.
3. Click **Change mandate** to authorize a different account.

## What happens next

- **Change mandate** takes you to the payment page to authorize the new account.

## Limitations

- Without a subscription, you see "No subscription has been started yet." First start a subscription under [Subscription](/en/settings/organization/subscription).
