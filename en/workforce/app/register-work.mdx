## What this page is for

Here you register in the app what you did on a day: your activities, times and trip details.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-werk-registreren.png" alt="Register work, Date step" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker who uses the app.

## How to use this page

1. On **Home**, tap **Register work**, or tap `+` at the bottom.
2. Under **Type**, choose **Productive (work)**.
3. Tap **Date** and choose the day. Tap **Save** and then **Next**.
4. Under **Workday / pattern**, choose a template, or tap **Create a manual registration** and choose your team. Tap **Next**.
5. Under **Work activities**, tap **Add activity** and choose what you did, for example **One-day trip**.
6. Fill in the activity's fields. Add more activities if needed.
7. Fill in **Break (minutes)**, optionally a **Memo**, and add your **Signature**.
8. Tap **Complete**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Type** | **Productive (work)** for hours worked. For leave, you choose **Non-productive**, see [Register leave](/en/workforce/app/register-leave). |
| **Date** | The day of the registration. In the calendar you choose a **Day**, a **Period** or separate **Days**. |
| **Start**, **End** | The activity's start and end time. |
| **Vehicle**, **Trailers** | The vehicle and trailers you drove with. |
| **Odometer (start)**, **Odometer (end)** | The odometer reading at departure and arrival. |
| **Trip number**, **Fuel added**, **AdBlue added**, **Toll** | The details of your trip. |
| **Memo** | A remark on the registration. |
| **Break (minutes)** | Your break in minutes. |
| **Signature** | Add your signature with your finger. |

<Hint kind="grey">Which fields you see and which are required depends on the activity and on what your employer has set for your team.</Hint>

## What happens next

- The registration is in [Overview](/en/workforce/app/overview) and waits for your employer's approval.
- If something is missing, you see under the field what you still need to fill in, for example "Fill in a start time."

## Limitations

- If you are not finished yet, tap `⋮` and **Save as draft**. With `Opnieuw beginnen`, you start the registration over.
- If the pay period of that day has already been processed, you see "This registration cannot be changed because the pay period has already been approved or processed." <Hint kind="yellow">Contact your employer then.</Hint>
