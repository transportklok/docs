## What this page is for

Here you turn extra features on or off for one team.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings**, choose the team, choose **Configuration and features** and open the **Features** tab.
2. Click **Add feature** and choose **Activate**, or click **Deactivate** next to an active feature.
3. Next to a feature, click **Settings** to set up that feature.

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Name** | The feature. |
| **Description** | What the feature does. |
| **Uses** | Which module the feature needs. |
| **Cost (per worker per month)** | What the feature costs per worker per month, or **Free**. |

## What happens next

- An activated feature is available to the team. For a scheduled change, you see `Wordt actief vanaf` or `Wordt inactief vanaf` with the date. With **Stop activation** or **Stop deactivation**, you undo it.

## Limitations

- A feature uses a module of the organization. See [Modules](/en/settings/organization/configuration/modules).
