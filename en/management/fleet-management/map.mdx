## What this page is for

This page helps you view vehicles on a map and in a list. That way you see more quickly which vehicle you are looking for.

## What you can do here

- Recognize vehicles by name and license plate.
- Decide from the map view which vehicle you want to open next.

## Screenshot

![Map](/images/en/captures/management-wagenpark-kaart.png)

## What you see on the page

- A map section with vehicle markers.
- A list of vehicle names and license plates next to or below the map.

## How to use this page

1. Open the **Map** page.
2. Find the vehicle you need in the list.
3. Use the map to assess the position or spread of vehicles.
4. Then continue to **Vehicles** if you want to change details.

## What happens next

- You get a quicker picture of which vehicle is where or belongs where.
- You can then go more directly to the right vehicle screen.

## Good to know

- This page is mainly meant for overview and recognition.
- To change vehicle details, use the vehicles section.
