## What this page is for

With the Samsara integration, TransportKlok fetches vehicles and positions from Samsara.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.
- The organization needs the **Fleet management** module, and telematics must be on. See [Integrations](/en/settings/organization/integrations/instances).

## How to use this page

1. Under **Accounts**, create an account for **Samsara** with a **Token** (API token) from Samsara.
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Samsara**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. Fill in the settings and click **Save settings**.

## Fields

On the settings page you fill in:

| Setting | What you fill in |
|------------|--------------|
| **Employee number attribute** | The Samsara driver attribute that holds the employee number. |
| **Start document type** | The document type in Samsara that starts a time registration. |
| **Stop document type** | The document type in Samsara that stops a time registration. |

## What happens next

| Function | Runs automatically |
|---------|---------------|
| **Synchronize vehicles** | every night |
| **Synchronize drivers** | every night |
| **Synchronize vehicle assignments** | every quarter of an hour |
| **Receive telemetry** | every quarter of an hour |

- Vehicles are linked on the chassis number (VIN).
- Positions appear on the [Map](/en/management/fleet-management/map).
- Until the settings are filled in, the integration shows **Configuration required**.
- If a driver in Samsara fills in the start document or stop document, TransportKlok starts or stops a time registration for that worker.

## Limitations

- The integration only fetches data from Samsara. It does not send anything back.
