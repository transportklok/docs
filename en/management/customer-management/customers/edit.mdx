## What this page is for

With this page you change the details of an existing client. The fields are similar to the create page.

## What you can do here

- Update the name, reference and contact details.

## Screenshot

![Edit client](/images/en/captures/management-opdrachtgevers-aanmaken.png)

## What you see on the page

- A form with the same main fields as when creating.

## How to use this page

1. Open an existing client from the overview.
2. Change the details that have changed.
3. Check the contact details once more.
4. Save the changes.

## What happens next

- The changed details are used in the overview and in linked processes.

## Good to know

- In practice, use the overview as your starting point to open a real client.
