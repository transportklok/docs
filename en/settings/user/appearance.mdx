## What this page is for

Here you choose the language in which TransportKlok is shown.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Appearance**.
2. Choose the **Language**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Language** | The language of the platform, for example `Nederlands`. |

## What happens next

- TransportKlok shows menus and buttons in the chosen language.

## Limitations

- The language only applies to your own account.
