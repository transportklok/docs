## What this page is for

On the **Accounts** tab, you record how TransportKlok logs in to your payroll package or telematics system. An integration under [Integrations](/en/settings/organization/integrations/instances) uses such an account.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Integrations** and choose the **Accounts** tab.
2. Click **Create Account**.
3. Under **Integration**, choose your system and fill in a **Name**.
4. Fill in the login details the system asks for, and click **Create Account**.
5. For a payroll package, you go to that package's login page. Log in there and give permission.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Integration** | The payroll package or telematics system. |
| **Name** | A name by which you recognize the account. |
| Login details | What the system asks for. |

The login details per telematics system:

| System | Log in with |
|---------|--------------|
| Webfleet, FleetGo, Verizon Connect | **Username** and **Password** |
| Samsara, Geotab, Linqo | **Token** |
| CO3 | **Client ID** and **Client secret** |

A payroll package asks for no login details here: you log in on the package's login page.

## What happens next

- The account is **Connected**. You then choose it for an integration.

## Limitations

- **Disconnect account** also deletes all integrations that use that account. <Hint kind="red">After disconnecting, TransportKlok no longer fetches or sends anything through those integrations.</Hint>
