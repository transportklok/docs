## What this page is for

With a work pattern, you decide which activities a group of workers registers on a workday. That way a driver registers different things than someone in the office.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings**, choose the team and choose **Work patterns**.
2. Create a new workday.
3. Fill in the **Name**, choose the **Activities** and indicate whether the workday is **Repeatable**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. With **Edit** you change a work pattern, with **Delete** you remove it.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Name** | The name of the workday. |
| **Activities** | The activities that belong to this workday. |
| **Repeatable** | Whether the workday can be registered several times in a row. |

## What happens next

- With the work pattern, workers only register the activities that are in it.
- A deleted work pattern is on the **Trash** tab. With **Restore**, you put it back.

## Limitations

- A work pattern applies within the team in which you create it.
