## What this page is for

Here you turn on two-factor authentication. TransportKlok then also asks for a code from an app on your phone when you log in.

## Screenshot

![Two-factor authentication](/images/en/captures/account-tweefactorauthenticatie.png)

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Authentication and security**.
2. Open the **Two-factor authentication** tab and click **Enable**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator. Or copy the **Setup key** to the app.
4. Fill in the 6-digit **Authenticator code** from the app and click **Confirm setup**.
5. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place. <Hint kind="red">With a recovery code you get into your account if you lose your phone. Without a recovery code, that is not possible.</Hint>

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| Status | Whether two-factor authentication is on. |
| **Show recovery codes** | Shows your recovery codes. |
| **Regenerate recovery codes** | Creates new recovery codes. |
| **Show QR code** | Shows the QR code again. |
| **Disable** | Turns two-factor authentication off. |

## What happens next

- When you log in, TransportKlok also asks for a code from the app. See [Logging in with two-factor authentication](/en/sign-in/two-factor).
- After turning it on, you are logged out on your other devices and browsers. After that, you log in there again with the code.

## Limitations

- For recovery codes, the QR code and disabling, TransportKlok first asks for your password.
