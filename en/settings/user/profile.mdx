## What this page is for

Here you decide how colleagues see you: your name and your profile photo.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings**.
2. Choose **General**.
3. Click **Select a new photo** to choose a profile photo.
4. Change your **Name**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Profile photo** | A photo by which colleagues recognize you. |
| **Name** | The name on your profile and in messages to colleagues. |

## What happens next

- Your photo and name appear everywhere in TransportKlok where you are shown.

## Limitations

- You change your email address and password under [Email address](/en/settings/user/account/email) and [Password](/en/settings/user/account/password).
