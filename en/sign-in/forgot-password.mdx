## What this page is for

If you have lost your password, you set a new one here through an email.

## Who can use this

- Everyone with a TransportKlok account.

## How to use this page

1. On the login page, click **Forgot your password?**.
2. Fill in your **Email** and click **Email password reset link**.
3. Open the email and click the link.
4. Fill in a new **Password**, confirm it and click **Reset password**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Email** | The email address of your account. |
| **Password**, **Confirm password** | Your new password, twice. |

## What happens next

- You see "Your password has been reset!" and log in with the new password. See [Log in](/en/sign-in/log-in).

## Limitations

- A reset link works for a limited time. If the link has expired, request a new one.
