## What this page is for

A detail page opens when you open an existing registration. There you check the full content of one registration.

## What you can do here

- Read through the full registration.
- Check that the registration is correct before you continue with changing or follow-up.

## Screenshot

![Time registration details](/images/en/captures/management-tijdregistratie-overzicht.png)

## What you see on the page

- An existing registration that is opened from the overview.
- The context of period, worker and registration details.

## How to use this page

1. First open an existing registration from the overview.
2. Check the worker, date and content of the registration.
3. Only continue to editing if something really needs to be changed.

## What happens next

- You know whether the registration is complete and correct.
- You can then go to editing or the timeline if needed.

## Good to know

- In practice, always use an existing registration from the overview as your starting point.
