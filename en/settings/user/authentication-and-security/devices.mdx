## What this page is for

Here you see which devices, such as the app on your phone, are signed in to your account, and you revoke a device.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Authentication and security**.
2. Open the **Devices** tab.
3. Click **Revoke** next to the device and confirm with your password.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| Device | A device that is signed in to your account, such as the app. |
| Sessions | The number of active sessions of the device. |

## What happens next

- A revoked device has to log in again.
