## What this page is for

On this page you change the details of an existing trailer.

## What you can do here

- Change the registration details.
- Check and save the trailer details.

## Screenshot

![Edit trailer](/images/en/captures/management-trailers-bewerken.png)

## What you see on the page

- The **Edit trailer** screen.
- Trailer details that are already filled in, such as country and license plate.
- A form that looks like the create page.

## How to use this page

1. Open the trailer from the overview.
2. Change the details that have changed.
3. Check the filled-in values once more.
4. Save the change.

## What happens next

- The changed trailer information is used right away in the overview and in linked processes.

## Good to know

- Only change details that have really changed.
- Afterwards, check that the trailer is still correctly recognizable in the overview.
