## What this page is for

Do you work for more than one organization, or have you received an invitation? Here you choose which organization you work with.

## Screenshot

<img src="/images/en/captures/app-organisaties.png" alt="Organizations in the app" width="320" />

## Who can use this

- Every worker who uses the app.

## How to use this page

1. Tap **Settings** and then **Organization and teams**.
2. Next to the organization you want to work with, tap **Open**.
3. If you have an invitation, tap **Accept** next to that organization.

## Fields

| Item | What you see |
|-----------|------------|
| Organization | The organization's name and your role, for example **Worker**. |
| **Open** | You continue in this organization. |
| **Accept** | You accept this organization's invitation. |
| **Log out** | You log out on this phone. |

## What happens next

- After **Open**, you see the **Home** of that organization.
- After **Accept**, the organization is in the list and you can open it.

## Limitations

- The app is for workers. If you are not a worker at any organization, you see "You are not a worker of an organization".
