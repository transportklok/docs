## What this page is for

If a time registration is not correct, you reject it. Here you read how that works and where to find the rejected registrations.

## Who can use this

- An administrator, or a manager for the teams the manager belongs to.

## How to use this page

Reject a registration:

1. Open the registration from the [Time registration overview](/en/management/hour-management/time-registrations/overview).
2. Under **Actions**, click **Reject**. You see `Registratie van … afkeuren`.
3. Change the registration to how it should be and finish.

View the rejected registrations:

1. Click the button with the cross at the top right. The red number on the button is the number of rejected registrations.
2. Click a registration in the list to open it.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| Number on the button | The number of registrations with the status **Rejected**. |
| Line in the list | The worker and the day of the rejected registration. |
| `Totaal … afgekeurde registraties` | The number of registrations in the list. |

## What happens next

- The registration gets the status **Rejected** and appears in the list behind the button.
- Anyone who has the notification **Time entry rejected** turned on receives a message. See [Notifications](/en/settings/organization/notifications).
- For a rejected registration, the **Change** button appears under **Actions**.

## Limitations

- You can only reject a registration that is **Open**.
- The list only shows registrations of workers with a running contract.
