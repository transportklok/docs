## What this page is for

With the AFAS Profit integration, you send workers, contracts and processed payroll runs to AFAS Profit.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization, with an AFAS Profit account that has access to the environment.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Create an account for **AFAS Profit** and log in to AFAS. See [Accounts](/en/settings/organization/integrations/accounts).
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **AFAS Profit**.
3. Choose the **Account**, give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
4. Fill in the connectors and click **Save settings**.
5. On **Sync behavior**, turn on the switches for what you want to send, such as **Send payroll runs**, and save.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Employees connector** | The name of the AFAS GetConnector for worker data, for example `Employees`. |
| **Engagement data connector** | The name of the AFAS GetConnector for employment data. |

## What happens next

- After **Save settings**, TransportKlok sends the workers to AFAS Profit.
- If you create or change a worker or contract, TransportKlok sends that data to AFAS Profit.
- When a payroll run is **Sent**, TransportKlok enters a mutation per pay component per worker in AFAS Profit, for the year and period of the run.

## Limitations

- Only pay components with an AFAS code are sent. <Hint kind="yellow">Which codes are set depends on how the integration is set up for your organization. After the first submission, check in AFAS Profit that the mutations are there.</Hint>
- The integration fetches nothing from AFAS Profit.
