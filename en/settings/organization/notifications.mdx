## What this page is for

Here you decide who gets which message, for example who is notified when a time registration is ready for approval.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Notifications**.
2. Click **Add recipient**.
3. Choose the **Notification**, the **Target** and the **Recipient**, and tick the **Channels**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. With **Edit** you change a recipient, with **Delete** you remove one.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Notification** | The message, see below. |
| **Target** | **User** or **Email**. |
| **Recipient** or **Email Address** | An administrator or manager, or a separate email address. |
| **Channels** | How the message arrives, such as **Email**. |

The messages:

| Notification | When |
|-------------|---------|
| **Time entry proposed** | A new time registration is ready to be approved. |
| **Time entry rejected** | A time registration has been rejected. |
| **Copy of exports when a payroll run is processed** | A payroll run has been processed. You receive a copy of the export files. This message belongs to the **Payroll inputs** module. |

## What happens next

- The recipient is in the list with **Notification**, **Target** and **Channels**, and receives the message in future.

## Limitations

- You can only set the same message for the same recipient once.
- This list applies to the whole organization. It is not a personal setting.
