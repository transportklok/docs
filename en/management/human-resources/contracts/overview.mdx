## What this page is for

Under **Contracts** you see the contracts of all your workforce together, by type of employment: employees, freelancers (ZZP) and charters.

## Screenshot

![Worker contracts](/images/en/captures/management-hrm-contracten.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** and choose **Contracts**.
2. In the menu under **Contracts**, choose the list: **Worker contracts**, **Self-employed (ZZP) contracts** or **Charter contracts**.
3. Click **Archive** to see the contracts that have already ended.
4. Click **Edit** next to a contract to change that worker's contract. See [Edit worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/edit).

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Name** | The worker, with personnel number and email address. Click the name to open the worker. |
| **Start** | The date on which the contract begins. |
| **End** | The date on which the contract ends, or `Doorlopend`. |
| **Hours** | Only for employee contracts: the contract hours. |
| **Annual hours cap** | Only for employee contracts: the maximum number of hours, or `Geen limiet`. |
| `Uurgarantie` | Only for employee contracts: how many weeks of pay are guaranteed, with the number of hours. |

## What happens next

- After **Edit**, you go to the worker's **Contracts** tab. There you create a contract, change it or set an end date.

## Limitations

- On this page you do not create or end a contract. You do that on the worker itself. See [Create worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/create).
- Without **Archive**, you only see contracts that are still running or that end today or later.
- You only see workers with an active license. <Hint kind="grey">You see the columns **Hours**, **Annual hours cap** and `Uurgarantie` if your organization uses the payroll processing module.</Hint>
