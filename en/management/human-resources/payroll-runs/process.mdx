## What this page is for

Here you read how to take a payroll run from draft to processed: checking, locking and completing.

## Who can use this

- An administrator with full rights in the organization.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Open **HR** → **Payroll administration** → **Payroll runs**.
2. Check the draft. Open the details with the icon in front of the number, or use **Download**.
3. If registrations have changed since, choose **Options** → **Run again** and wait until the status is **Draft** again.
4. Click **Lock** and confirm in the **Lock payroll run** window.
5. Wait until the status is **Locked**.
6. Click **Complete**.
7. If the **Carry corrections** window appears, choose which older corrections the run carries. See [Corrections](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-runs/corrections).

## Fields

Completing has no fields to fill in. Without older corrections, the run is processed right away, without an extra window.

## What happens next

- The run gets the status **Processed**.
- Under **Download** you now find the **Payroll run export**. See [Exports of a payroll run](/en/management/human-resources/payroll-runs/exports).
- Anyone who has **Copy of exports when a payroll run is processed** turned on in the notifications receives an email with a copy of the export.

## Limitations

- You cannot undo processing with a button.
- You can no longer delete a processed run.
- If you still want to change a locked run, choose **Options** → **Unlock**.
- If your organization takes the variable pay components from the previous period, and that previous period has no run, the **Previous period without a payroll run** window asks you to confirm that the period has nothing to pay. A run's detail page shows the **Source period for variable pay components**.
- What the calculation produces depends on the registrations and the settings. This depends on the CAO or the contract setting: which pay components a worker gets follows from the CAO and from the worker's contract.
