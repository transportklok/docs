## What this page is for

The timeline page shows how a registration has developed over time or which steps are linked to it.

## What you can do here

- View the order of events or changes.

## Screenshot

![Time registration timeline](/images/en/captures/management-tijdregistratie-overzicht.png)

## What you see on the page

- A timeline that you open from an existing registration.

## How to use this page

1. First open an existing registration.
2. Then open that registration's timeline.
3. Read the events from top to bottom or from recent to old.

## What happens next

- You get more context on what has happened to a registration.

## Good to know

- Always use a real existing registration from your overview to open this page.
