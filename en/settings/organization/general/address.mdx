## What this page is for

Here you record your organization's main address and phone number. This address appears on the invoices.

## Who can use this

- Everyone in the organization can view the details. Only an administrator can save them.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **General**.
2. Open the **Address** tab and fill in the address.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Street**, **Postal code**, **City** | The main address. This address appears on the invoices. |
| **Phone number** | The phone number, with the country code in front. |

## What happens next

- After **Save**, you see **Saved.** The new address applies to the next invoice.

## Limitations

- Only an administrator can change these details. For everyone else, **Save** is disabled.
