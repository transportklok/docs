## What this page is for

Here you see which external apps have access to your account through OAuth, and you revoke that access again.

## Who can use this

- Every user, for their own account.

## How to use this page

1. At the top right, click your initials and choose **Settings** → **Apps**.
2. For each app, see when you gave access, for which organization, with which permissions and when the app last did something.
3. Click **Unlink** to revoke an app's access, and confirm.
4. With **Marketplace**, you see which apps are available for your organization.

## Fields

| What you see | Meaning |
|------------|-----------|
| `Geautoriseerd op` | When you gave the app access. |
| **Organization** | For which organization the app has access. |
| **Permissions** | What the app may do. |
| `Laatst gebruikt op` | When the app last did something, or **Not used yet**. |

## What happens next

- After **Unlink**, the app no longer has access. <Hint kind="red">If you want to use the app again, you give permission again from that app.</Hint>

## Limitations

- If there is no app yet, you see "You have not granted any apps OAuth access yet."
