## The limits

The API counts requests per access token. A token may not do more than the following at the same time:

| Period | Requests |
|---------|-----------|
| 1 minute | 180 |
| 5 minutes | 600 |
| 1 hour | 3,600 |

## When you reach a limit

- The API responds with status `429 Too Many Requests`.
- The `Retry-After` header tells you after how many seconds you can try again.
- The `X-RateLimit-Limit` and `X-RateLimit-Remaining` headers show how many requests are allowed in the period and how many are left.

## How to stay under the limit

- After a `429`, wait at least as long as `Retry-After` indicates.
- Keep what you have already fetched instead of requesting everything again each time.
