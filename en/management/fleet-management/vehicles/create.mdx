## What this page is for

With this form you add a new vehicle to the fleet.

## What you can do here

- Fill in general vehicle details.
- Record links, such as additional vehicle relations.

## Screenshot

![Create vehicle](/images/en/captures/management-voertuigen-aanmaken.png)

## What you see on the page

- The **Create vehicle** screen.
- Sections such as **General** and **Assignments**.
- Fields for, among other things, the country of registration and vehicle identification.

## How to use this page

1. Open **New vehicle** from the overview.
2. First fill in the general vehicle details.
3. Check the registration details carefully.
4. Then continue with the other fields or links.
5. Click **Create** when everything is correct.

## What happens next

- The vehicle is added to your fleet.
- You then see the vehicle in the vehicles overview.

## Good to know

- Use official vehicle details from the registration certificate.
- Only save once the required fields are filled in.
