## What this page is for

Here you see who has received an invitation and has not accepted it yet. You resend an invitation, withdraw it, or create a link with which people create an account themselves.

## Screenshot

![Invitations](/images/en/captures/management-personeel-uitnodigingen.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization.

## How to use this page

Send an invitation:

1. Create a worker with `Met gebruikersaccount` and an **Email**. See [Create worker](/en/management/human-resources/workers/create).
2. The invitation goes to that email address right away.

Open invitations:

1. Open **HR** → **Workers** and click **Invitations**.
2. Click **Resend** to send the email once more.
3. Click **Cancel** to withdraw an invitation, and confirm with **Delete**.
4. Click **Edit** to change the worker, for example a wrong email address.

An open invitation link:

1. Next to **Open invitation link**, click **Create link**.
2. Click **Copy** and share the link, for example via WhatsApp.
3. Anyone with the link creates an account themselves with their own email address. <Hint kind="red">Anyone who has the link can sign up to your organization. Withdraw a link if you no longer use it.</Hint>
4. Under **Invitation links** you see all active links. With **Copy link** you copy a link again, with **Revoke** you turn it off.

## Fields

| Column | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|
| **Name** | The worker's name. |
| **Email** | The address the invitation was sent to. |
| **Invited on** | When the invitation was sent. |

## What happens next

- The worker receives an email with the **Accept Invitation** button. After accepting, the invitation disappears from the list.
- In the overview, for a worker with an open invitation, `Kopier uitnodiging` also appears under **Actions**.

## Limitations

- The invitation can only be accepted with an account on the same email address.
- A withdrawn invitation no longer works. If you still want to invite the worker, change the email address via **Edit**.
