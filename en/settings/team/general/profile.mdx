## What this page is for

Here you record a team's name.

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the team.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** and choose the team.
2. Choose **General** and open the **Profile** tab.
3. Change the **Name** and click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Name** | The team's name. |

## What happens next

- The new name appears in the menu and in the overviews.

## Limitations

- You only see **General** if your organization has more than one team.
