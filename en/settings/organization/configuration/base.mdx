## What this page is for

Here you set how TransportKlok works for your whole organization.

## Who can use this

- An administrator of the organization.

## How to use this page

1. Open **Settings** → **Organization settings** → **Configuration and modules**.
2. On the **Basic configuration** tab, change the settings.
3. Click **Save**.

## Fields

| Field | What you choose |
|------|-------------|
| **File type for data exports** | The file type of your exports: **Excel (xlsx)** or **CSV**. See [Exporting](/en/general/exports). |

## What happens next

- The setting applies to the whole organization. A team has the same setting under [Team basic configuration](/en/settings/team/configuration/base).
