## What this page is for

Here, as an owner or manager, you create a new TransportKlok account for your organization.

## Who can use this

- An owner or manager of an organization that does not have a TransportKlok account yet.

## How to use this page

1. On the login page, click **Create an account**.
2. Under **Account type**, choose **Owner / Manager** and click **Next**.
3. Under **Account**, fill in your name, email address and password, and agree to the general terms and the privacy policy.
4. Under **Organization**, fill in your organization's name, address and phone number.
5. Under **Modules**, choose the subscription and the modules you need.
6. Finish the registration.

## Fields

| Step | Field | What you fill in |
|------|------|--------------|
| **Account** | **Name** | Your name. |
| **Account** | **Email Address** | The address you log in with. |
| **Account** | **Password**, **Confirm password** | At least 8 characters, with at least 1 capital letter, 1 digit and 1 special character. |
| **Organization** | **Organization name**, **Address**, **Phone number** | Your organization's details. |
| **Modules** | Subscription and modules | See [Subscription](/en/settings/organization/subscription). |

## What happens next

- You see "You have successfully created an account!" and you are the administrator of the new organization, with a trial period.
- You receive an email to confirm your email address. Only then can you continue. See [Confirm email address](/en/sign-in/confirm-email).

## Limitations

- Are you a worker? Then you do not create an account here. You receive an invitation from your organization. See [Accept invitation](/en/sign-in/accept-invitation).
- Registering and logging in only works in the Chrome browser.
