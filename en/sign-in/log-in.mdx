## What this page is for

Here you read how to log in to TransportKlok.

## Who can use this

- Everyone with a TransportKlok account.

## How to use this page

1. Open the TransportKlok login page in Chrome.
2. Fill in your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Remember me** if you want to stay logged in on this device.
4. Click **Log in**.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Email** | The email address of your account. |
| **Password** | Your password. |
| **Remember me** | Whether you stay logged in on this device. |

## What happens next

- After **Log in**, you go to TransportKlok.
- If two-factor authentication is on, TransportKlok first asks for a code. See [Logging in with two-factor authentication](/en/sign-in/two-factor).
- If you have not yet confirmed your email address, you first see **Email Verification**. See [Confirm email address](/en/sign-in/confirm-email).

## Limitations

- Logging in only works in the Chrome browser. In another browser you see "Browser not allowed." and **Log in** is disabled.
- Have you lost your password? See [Forgot password](/en/sign-in/forgot-password).
