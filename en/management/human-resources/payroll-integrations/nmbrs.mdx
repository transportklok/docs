## What this page is for

With the Nmbrs integration, you send processed payroll runs to Nmbrs and fetch workers, contracts and leave from Nmbrs.

## Screenshot

![Connect Nmbrs](/images/en/captures/instellingen-integraties-nmbrs-koppelen.png)

## Who can use this

- An administrator or manager of the organization, with a Nmbrs account that has access to the employer.
- The organization needs the **Payroll inputs** module.

## How to use this page

1. Create an account for **Nmbrs** and log in to Nmbrs. See [Accounts](/en/settings/organization/integrations/accounts).
2. Open **Integrations**, click **Add integration** and click **Connect** next to **Nmbrs**.
3. Choose the **Account**.
4. Choose the **Employer** from the Nmbrs list.
5. Give the integration a **Name** and click **Connect**.
6. Fill in the settings and click **Save settings**.
7. On **Sync behavior**, turn on the switch next to **Send payroll runs** and save.

## Fields

| Field | What you fill in |
|------|--------------|
| **Employer** (required) | The company in Nmbrs. You cannot choose an employer that is already linked. |
| **Create workers automatically** | If this is off, TransportKlok only links workers with the same personnel number and the same last name. |
| **Pay code mappings** | Which Nmbrs pay code belongs to which kind of hours and allowance. <Hint kind="yellow">This depends on how your Nmbrs company is set up. Agree on the codes with whoever does your payroll administration.</Hint> |
| **Nmbrs workers for payroll import** | The Nmbrs workers that take part, separated by commas or lines. If you leave this empty, all linked workers take part. |
| **Nmbrs leave group mappings** | Which leave group in Nmbrs belongs to which kind of leave. |

## What happens next

- When a payroll run is **Sent**, TransportKlok enters the hours and pay components of that period per worker in Nmbrs, and adds the payslip overview as a PDF. Corrections that are carried along go with it.
- Data that TransportKlok previously entered in Nmbrs for that period is updated or removed, so that Nmbrs has the latest processed version.
- Every night, TransportKlok fetches workers, contracts and leave from Nmbrs.
- Under **Sync behavior** → **Runs**, you see for each submission whether it is **Succeeded** or **Failed**.

## Limitations

- The Nmbrs company must work with monthly periods. <Hint kind="yellow">This depends on how your Nmbrs company is set up.</Hint>
- Workers who are not linked to the chosen employer in Nmbrs are skipped by the submission.
- A submission fails, among other things, if access to Nmbrs has expired, if no employer is chosen, if a pay component has no Nmbrs code, or if that period in Nmbrs already contains data from another run or integration.
